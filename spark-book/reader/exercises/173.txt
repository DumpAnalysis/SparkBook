Exercise 173  Create inspectable image and audio fixtures

Question
Can we identify the exact pixels and sound samples before invoking a model?

Before you start
Exercise 172 (qualify a separate media environment)
Download one pinned model at a time. Qualify media dependencies; measure sequence, resolution and offload headroom.

Recorded step time
1.3 s for Fresh media fixtures (record 173.01).

Check for success
Decode the fixtures and verify dimensions, sample counts and hashes. Check that malformed inputs fail at the decoder.

Run in your lab
Begin with the entry procedure below, then complete the variations and acceptance checks in the chapter.

: "${LAB_RUN:?Run source reader/new_run.sh first}"

python media/controls.py fixtures --out "$LAB_RUN/media-fixtures"

Interpret the result
Separate signal validity, task quality, listening/visual review and profiler availability.

If the check fails
Check decoding/preprocessing before model behavior; isolate one pipeline stage.

Finish
Keep $LAB_RUN/media-fixtures with the command and exit that produced it.

Handoff
Record the absolute paths and identities of any selected output before changing terminals or starting a dependent exercise.
