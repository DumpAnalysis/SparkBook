Exercise 88  Run a deliberately wrong-pair control

Question
Would the evaluation notice if the positive training documents were deliberately wrong?

Before you start

Check for success

Interpret the result
Evaluate retrieval in the original space; a map or lower loss is not a quality pass.

If the check fails
